Delete your account

You can permanently delete your NerdMaxxing account here. Sign in with the same Google account you use in the app so we can verify which account to remove.

This does not delete your Google account. Limited infrastructure backups or records required by law may remain subject to the retention periods described in our Privacy Policy.
