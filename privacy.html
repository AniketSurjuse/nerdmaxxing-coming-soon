Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NerdMaxxing.

NerdMaxxing is a social learning and self-improvement platform that encourages people to develop skills, take on challenges, track their progress and grow alongside a community of like-minded individuals.

This Privacy Policy explains how NerdMaxxing collects, uses, stores, processes and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application, website and related services.

NerdMaxxing is currently operated by Aniket Surjuse, an independent individual developer, based in India. There is no separate incorporated company operating the service at this time.

By creating an account or using NerdMaxxing, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate and improve the service.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, NerdMaxxing may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and authentication information

To create a NerdMaxxing account, you must sign up using Google Sign-In. When you do, we receive certain information from Google, which may include:

  • Email address.
  • Name associated with your Google account.
  • Google profile picture.
  • Authentication identifiers required to establish and maintain your NerdMaxxing account.

We never receive, see or store your Google account password. Google Sign-In is handled entirely by Google.

After signing up, you can choose how to log in to your account in future: either with Google, or with your NerdMaxxing username and a password. A username and password can only be used to log in. They cannot be used to sign up.

We do not offer a separate password reset feature. If you forget your NerdMaxxing password, you can get back into your account at any time by logging in with Google.

We use this information to authenticate you, create your account and provide access to the platform.

2.2 Profile information

When using NerdMaxxing, you may provide information such as:

  • Username.
  • Display name.
  • Biography.
  • Profile picture or avatar.
  • Achievements and other profile-related information.

This information helps other members identify you and understand your activity on the platform.

2.3 Challenge and activity information

To provide challenge and progress-tracking functionality, we collect information such as:

  • Challenges you enroll in.
  • Challenge completion status.
  • Completed challenges.
  • Achievement records.
  • Progress and verification status.
  • Challenge-related activity.
  • Aura points, rankings and leaderboard participation, where applicable.

Visibility: Other signed-in NerdMaxxing users can view completed challenges and associated publicly displayed achievements. Challenges you are attempting or currently working on are not visible to other users.

2.4 Social information

NerdMaxxing includes community features that may collect and display:

  • Followers and following relationships.
  • Squad memberships.
  • Leaderboard participation.
  • Public profile activity and achievements.

This information supports the social and community features of NerdMaxxing.

2.5 Challenge evidence

Some challenges may require you to submit evidence to demonstrate completion.

Depending on the challenge, submitted evidence may contain:

  • Text descriptions.
  • Images.
  • Videos.
  • Other challenge-related supporting material.

Challenge evidence is intended for private verification and is not displayed as public profile content.

The developer may access submitted evidence for manual verification, moderation, troubleshooting or security purposes.

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information or content belonging to other individuals without their permission.

2.6 Technical and operational information

Our application and infrastructure providers may process certain technical information required to operate the service. Depending on the provider and interaction, this may include:

  • IP address.
  • Device and application information.
  • Authentication and session information.
  • Server and request logs.
  • Push notification registration tokens.
  • Error and operational diagnostic information.

We do not currently use a dedicated analytics platform to track user behaviour or build advertising profiles.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Signing users up through Google Sign-In, and logging them in through Google or username and password.
  • Maintaining user profiles.
  • Providing challenges and tracking completion.
  • Displaying achievements and community activity.
  • Managing followers, squads and leaderboards.
  • Delivering push notifications.
  • Reviewing challenge evidence.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing misuse.
  • Troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Responding to privacy requests and support enquiries.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not currently offer direct messaging between users.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Information Visibility and Sharing Between Users

NerdMaxxing is designed as a community platform.

Information visibility currently works as follows:

Visible to all signed-in NerdMaxxing users:

  • Username and display name.
  • Biography and profile picture.
  • Achievements.
  • Completed challenges.
  • Followers and following relationships.
  • Squad memberships.
  • Leaderboard participation and associated rankings.

Not publicly visible to other users:

  • Challenges you are attempting.
  • Challenges currently in progress.
  • Private challenge evidence submitted for verification.

Only signed-in users can access the community information described above.

Please remember that information visible to other members may be viewed, recorded or shared by those members outside NerdMaxxing. We cannot control how other users handle information they can legitimately access.

We do not currently provide direct user-to-user messaging.

5. Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

NerdMaxxing relies on third-party providers to operate its application and infrastructure.

These providers may process personal information on our behalf or as independent service providers, depending on their role and applicable terms.

Our current infrastructure includes:

5.1 Google Sign-In

Google provides authentication services and shares the account information necessary to authenticate users. Google Sign-In is required to sign up for NerdMaxxing and is also available as a log-in option afterwards.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Vercel

Vercel provides backend hosting and application infrastructure.

Information processed through its services may include server requests, IP addresses and technical logs necessary to deliver and secure the service.

Vercel Privacy Policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

5.3 Neon

Neon provides PostgreSQL database infrastructure used to store and manage application information.

Information stored in the database may include account records, profile information, challenge activity and community-related records.

Neon Privacy Policy: https://neon.com/privacy-policy

5.4 Cloudflare R2

Cloudflare R2 provides object storage for files uploaded to NerdMaxxing, which may include profile images and challenge evidence.

Uploaded files are stored and processed through Cloudflare’s infrastructure to provide application functionality.

Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5.5 Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a Google service, is used to deliver push notifications.

This may involve processing device registration tokens and technical information required to deliver notifications.

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not currently integrate a dedicated advertising network, analytics platform or third-party crash-reporting platform.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies, contractual obligations and applicable laws.

6. Data Storage and International Processing

Your information is stored and processed using the infrastructure providers described in this policy.

These providers may operate servers or process information in different countries, depending on their infrastructure and service configurations.

Consequently, some information may be processed outside India.

We take reasonable steps to use service providers and infrastructure arrangements appropriate for operating NerdMaxxing and protecting user information.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide NerdMaxxing’s services and fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Information may be retained while your account remains active.

If you request account deletion, we delete your associated user data from our Neon database and Cloudflare R2 storage.

We do not intentionally maintain a separate user profile or retain a copy of your account data after the deletion process.

Certain technical records or residual copies maintained by infrastructure providers as part of their routine backup, security or operational systems may be subject to their respective retention and deletion cycles.

Where information must be retained to comply with applicable law, we will handle it in accordance with the relevant legal requirements.

8. Account Deletion

You have the right to stop using NerdMaxxing and request deletion of your account.

You can initiate account deletion through the account deletion functionality available within the application.

You can also request account deletion online. If you need help, contact:

Email: hello@nerdmaxxing.com

Upon account deletion, we delete the associated information under our control, including:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Challenge participation and completion records.
  • Achievements and activity records.
  • Followers and following relationships.
  • Squad memberships and related records.
  • Submitted challenge evidence.
  • Uploaded profile pictures and other associated files stored in Cloudflare R2.
  • Other user-associated records stored in our database.

We do not intentionally retain user-specific application data after completing the deletion process.

Deletion of your NerdMaxxing account does not delete your Google account. Google may continue to retain information associated with your Google account under its own privacy policy.

If you have questions about account deletion, contact us at hello@nerdmaxxing.com.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss and misuse.

These measures include using established infrastructure providers, authentication mechanisms and access controls.

However, no internet-based service, electronic storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy and Age Requirements

NerdMaxxing is currently intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older.

Individuals under the age of 18 are not permitted to create accounts or use the service under the current age policy.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

If we become aware that an account belongs to someone under the permitted age, we may suspend or delete the account and associated information.

If you believe an individual under 18 has created an account, please contact us at hello@nerdmaxxing.com.
